Shape locker — the cell, the shapes, the blocks Two levels of one mechanism: the resizable region is a cell; <blk-board> reads its shape and picks a slot grid. Each slot is a cell; the block dropped in reads its shape and picks its own form. Resize the region from its corner, or pick a preset, and both levels rearrange with no script involved. A part lists the shapes it belongs in (spot line rail · slip sheet · tile panel · strip banner); the gate layer shows it only there; the block's one rule arranges per shape with @container shape (…). Tick "Shape tags" to see each cell name its shape.
