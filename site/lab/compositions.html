Blocks — the self-managing cell (gf-608) .block is a SIZE container with one child, its body: a column in portrait, a row in landscape (wider than 3:2) — the words first, the big thing beside them. Inside it the tiers gate on the width (.small/.medium/.large), .c-portrait/.c-landscape on the orientation and .c-short/.c-tall on the height — the map stands down in a short cell, the address in a narrow one. A block never sizes itself: a grid track, a .frame (at any --ratio) or a .stress hands it its cell. Drag the stress corner both ways.
