THE SELECTION VOCABULARY (gf-191) Four spellings, four meanings — never interchangeable. aria-current="page" = REAL currentness (the org switcher's org, a step strip's station). aria-pressed = a toggle's on. aria-selected = a listbox's CHOSEN row — generalized into the .menu and .tabs paints (see it live on the combo-box rocket bench; a bare .menu can't render in flow by design). .active = the GLOBAL UTILITY for a transient client highlight (a combobox's keyboard row): paint without stolen semantics.
