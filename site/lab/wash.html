Wash — the page's tint A static, viewport-fixed underlay of two soft pools in the surface's own colour: the element's --bg resolves the first pool, a real child at the next ladder step and --hue-shift: 30 the second (the engine resolves --_bg per element, so a pseudo cannot carry its own hue). No blur, no motion; it re-themes with the hue and with day and night. The host of a wash becomes a stacking context (:has(> .wash) { isolation: isolate }) so the pools paint above its surface and under its content.
