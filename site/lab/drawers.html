Drawers — a panel that slides in from an edge A native <dialog class="drawer"> with one edge word — .left · .right · .top · .bottom — opened by showModal(), closed by closedby="any" (a click outside, Escape). The engine owns the slide, the scrim and the timing, all on the motion clock: set the toolbar's motion to Debug and watch the anatomy at a tenth of the speed; Off removes it. .glass frosts the scrim. .drawer-grow lets the panel take its content's height. On a live page every dialog carries data-preserve-attr="open", or a push morphs it shut.
